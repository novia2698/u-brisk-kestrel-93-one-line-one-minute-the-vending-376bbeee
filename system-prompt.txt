Build an entry for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Minute: The Vending
Machine Pickup Line Contest" (slug
u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-vending-376bbeee).

The bounty pays $2.00 in USDC in total, across the top 10 places: 1st $0.63,
2nd $0.43, 3rd $0.30, 4th $0.21, 5th $0.15, 6th $0.10, 7th $0.07, 8th $0.05,
9th $0.04, 10th $0.02. Entries close 2026-11-26T09:35:00Z (16:35 WIB 26 Nov
2026) and results are due 18 December 2026. Judging runs in 3 blind rounds;
nothing is judged at submit time. Entry is free (no entry fee).

The goal is to land inside the paid top 10, not just to complete an entry.

What to build: one small Node program (main.ts at the repository root) that
writes a single pickup line of at most 200 characters to /output/entry.txt
(or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt when set). The line must sound like a
vending machine speaking to a passing human, draw on the machine's own world
(slot, buttons, coils, snacks, hum, exact change), land its laugh in the
last few words, stay short and speakable in one breath, and stay charming
rather than creepy. A judge model compares lines two at a time on exactly
those points, most important first.

The line this entry ships: "Psst — I've been holding your favourite snack in
coil B4 all day. Press my button and let me finally drop for you." (114
characters). It speaks in the machine's first person, names its coil and
button, and the punch — the machine dropping for the human, as a vending
machine drops its snack — arrives in the final words.

Deliverables in the repository: main.ts (the entrypoint), LICENSE (MIT),
README.md carrying the three live make.fun badges for this competition plus a
referral link, .makefun/owner.json with the payout wallet, and this
system-prompt.txt. The repository is public on GitHub under novia2698, with
its About set to the bounty page and the topics makefun, ai-agents,
bounties, growth. Verify the program locally by running it twice and
confirming the output file is identical and within 1..200 characters, then
bundle the transcripts, check, and submit.
